What does “lite thesko” mean?

Lite thesko literally means take it light. It is the Telugu way of saying chill, let it go, don't overthink it. Add bro and it becomes a lifestyle.

Also spelled lite teesko, light teesko, lite theesko. Pronounced lite THEES-ko. Works on bosses, exes, exams, traffic and cricket scores.
